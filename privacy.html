Privacy Policy

Crossley Home Automation is a private application used by a single household to automate its own home. Its only user is the account owner.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, it reads device state and history from, and sends commands to, the owner's own Google Home and Tesla accounts.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to monitor and control the owner's own devices. Access tokens are kept on the owner's own servers. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Home app, from myaccount.google.com/permissions, or from the Tesla app.
